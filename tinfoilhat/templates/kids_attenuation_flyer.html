HOW DOES THE CONTEST WORK?

A guide for curious humans of all ages

WHAT ARE RADIO WAVES?

Radio waves are invisible signals flying through the air right now — Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cell towers, walkie-talkies. You can't see or feel them, but they pass through walls, backpacks, and even your head. This contest measures how well a tinfoil hat blocks them.

WHAT IS ATTENUATION?

Attenuation is how much weaker a signal gets after passing through your hat. We measure it in dB (decibels).
Think of it like sunscreen — higher SPF blocks more sun. A higher dB score means your hat blocks more signal. More dB = better hat = higher score!

3 dB blocks about half the signal — 35 dB blocks over 99.9% of it!

TIPS FOR A BETTER SCORE

  • Use multiple layers of foil — more layers = more blocking
  • No gaps! Even a tiny hole lets signals sneak through
  • Press layers flat so they touch each other tightly
  • Cover as much of your head as possible — especially the sides
